# Vaultify - Promo Video Script

**Target Duration:** 60 Seconds
**Tone:** Professional, Secure, and Productive.

---

## (0:00 - 0:10) Intro: The Chaos of Digital Content
**Visual:**
- Split screen showing YouTube, Instagram, and a cluttered Notes app.
- A user looking overwhelmed.
- Transition to the sleek **Vaultify** Homepage with the "Welcome" banner.
**Audio (Narrator):**
"Struggling to keep track of your digital life? Links scattered across chats, notes, and browser tabs?
Meet **Vaultify**. The ultimate workspace to organize, secure, and manage everything."

---

## (0:10 - 0:25) Feature 1: seamless Media Organization
**Visual:**
- Cursor copying a URL from YouTube.
- Pasting it into Vaultify -> Click "Video" -> Auto-fetch Title/Thumbnail -> Save.
- Shows the video appearing instantly in the "YouTube Videos" category.
- Quickly doing the same for an Instagram Reel.
**Audio (Narrator):**
"Stop losing your favorite content. Whether it's a YouTube tutorial, a Short, or an Instagram Reel, simply paste the link.
Vaultify automatically detects the platform, fetches the metadata, and categorizes it for you. Instant organization."

---

## (0:25 - 0:40) Feature 2: Client-Side Security (The Hero Feature)
**Visual:**
- User clicks the "🔒 Security" category. It requires a password.
- User types the password. Correct!
- A previously hidden list of "Secret Notes" appears.
- User clicks "Add Item" -> "Secret Note" -> Types a private memory -> Info pop-up: "Encrypted before saving".
**Audio (Narrator):**
"But what about your private data? Privacy is our priority.
With our military-grade Client-Side End-to-End Encryption, your secrets are locked *before* they even leave your device. Only YOU have the key."

---

## (0:40 - 0:50) Feature 3: Productivity Tools
**Visual:**
- User uploading a PDF file.
- A loading spinner... then "AI Summary" appears with key bullet points.
- User clicking the Calendar icon (📅).
- The calendar view opens, showing dots on days where items were saved.
**Audio (Narrator):**
"Vaultify is more than just storage. It's your productivity hub.
Upload PDFs and get instant AI summaries. view your activity on the interactive calendar to track your digital footprint."

---

## (0:50 - 1:00) Outro
**Visual:**
- Quick montage: Dark mode, Mobile view (PWA), "Wallet" balance processing.
- Fade to the **Vaultify Logo**.
- Text: "Secure. Smart. Organized."
**Audio (Narrator):**
"Secure. Smart. Organized.
Take control of your digital world.
**Vaultify**. Try it today."
